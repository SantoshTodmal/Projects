QUALITY INSPECTION AND SAMPLING SOP

    Purpose:
    To verify that manufactured products meet specifications.

    Procedure:
    1. Check the approved drawing and product specifications.
    2. Inspect the first produced component.
    3. Measure critical dimensions using approved instruments.
    4. Record inspection results.
    5. Perform in-process sampling according to the control plan.
    6. Identify and segregate nonconforming products.
    7. Inform the quality department about identified defects.
    8. Resume affected production only after required approval.

    Record:
    Maintain inspection results and defect records.
