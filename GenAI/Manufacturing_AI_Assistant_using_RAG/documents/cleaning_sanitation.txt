CLEANING AND SANITATION SOP

    Purpose:
    To maintain clean equipment and production areas.

    Procedure:
    1. Stop the equipment and make it safe for cleaning.
    2. Follow approved energy-isolation requirements where needed.
    3. Remove production residues using approved methods.
    4. Use authorized cleaning agents and tools.
    5. Inspect the cleaned equipment and work area.
    6. Record the cleaning activity.
    7. Obtain verification when required by the procedure.

    Safety:
    Follow chemical handling instructions and required PPE.
