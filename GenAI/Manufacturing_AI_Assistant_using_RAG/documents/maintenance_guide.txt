MACHINE MAINTENANCE GUIDE

    Purpose:
    To maintain equipment reliability and reduce unexpected downtime.

    Common Warning Signs:
    1. Unusual machine vibration.
    2. Abnormal noise during operation.
    3. Excessive temperature or overheating.
    4. Oil leakage or lubrication problems.
    5. Loose or visibly damaged components.
    6. Unexpected machine stoppages.

    Maintenance Procedure:
    1. Report the machine problem to the maintenance team.
    2. Stop the machine if continued operation may be unsafe.
    3. Isolate hazardous energy using the approved Lockout/Tagout
       (LOTO) procedure before applicable maintenance work.
    4. Allow hot components to cool where required.
    5. Inspect relevant components for wear or damage.
    6. Check lubrication according to the manufacturer's instructions.
    7. Replace or repair components only as authorized.
    8. Verify that tools and maintenance materials are removed.
    9. Restore guards and safety devices before restarting.
    10. Test the machine according to the approved procedure.
    11. Record the maintenance activity and downtime.

    Preventive Maintenance:
    1. Follow the approved maintenance schedule.
    2. Inspect critical machine components regularly.
    3. Check lubrication levels and leakage.
    4. Monitor vibration, temperature, and abnormal noise.
    5. Record inspection findings and completed activities.

    Safety Precautions:
    Only trained and authorized personnel may perform maintenance.
    Never bypass machine guards or safety interlocks.
    Never perform maintenance on equipment without the required
    energy isolation and verification.

    Maintenance Records:
    Record the machine ID, date, reported problem, action taken,
    replacement parts, downtime, and responsible technician.
