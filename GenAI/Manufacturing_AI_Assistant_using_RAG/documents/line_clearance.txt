LINE CLEARANCE AND CHANGEOVER SOP

    Purpose:
    To prevent material mix-ups during product changeovers.

    Procedure:
    1. Stop the previous production run.
    2. Remove previous product materials and labels.
    3. Inspect the production area for leftover components.
    4. Clean the equipment according to the approved procedure.
    5. Verify the next product and material identification.
    6. Complete the line-clearance checklist.
    7. Obtain required authorization before restarting production.

    Record:
    Document the product, batch, date, and clearance approval.
