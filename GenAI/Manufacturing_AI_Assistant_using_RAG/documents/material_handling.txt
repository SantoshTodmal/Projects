MATERIAL HANDLING AND STORAGE SOP

    Purpose:
    To maintain material identification, quality, and traceability.

    Procedure:
    1. Verify incoming material against delivery documents.
    2. Check material identification and quantity.
    3. Inspect for visible damage or packaging issues.
    4. Label and record accepted materials.
    5. Segregate unidentified or nonconforming materials.
    6. Store materials in designated locations.
    7. Follow applicable storage conditions and stock rotation.
    8. Record material issues to production.
    9. Update inventory records after material movements.

    Record:
    Maintain material receipt, issue, and stock information.
