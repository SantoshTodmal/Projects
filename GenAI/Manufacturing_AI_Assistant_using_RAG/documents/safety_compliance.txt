SAFETY AND COMPLIANCE SOP

    Purpose:
    To reduce workplace hazards during production.

    Procedure:
    1. Wear the required personal protective equipment.
    2. Inspect the workplace for visible hazards.
    3. Keep walkways and emergency exits unobstructed.
    4. Report unsafe conditions immediately.
    5. Stop work when an immediate danger is identified.
    6. Isolate hazardous energy before applicable maintenance.
    7. Follow site emergency response instructions.
    8. Record incidents and near misses according to site rules.

    Safety:
    Only trained and authorized personnel may perform
    specialized maintenance and energy-isolation activities.
